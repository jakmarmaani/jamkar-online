Parent Privacy

JamKar Online is designed so that the parent or guardian manages the family account. We collect the parent email address for sign-in, account recovery and access management. Child profiles use usernames and avatars and are kept separate from the parent email in the child-facing experience.

Authentication is handled through Supabase. Payment checkout is handled by Revolut. JamKar does not store raw parent passwords or payment-card details in the website code or browser storage. Game progress and account entitlement may be stored in the JamKar database so that family access can be restored after sign-in.

Parents should avoid using a child's full legal name, school name, address, phone number or other sensitive information as a child username.

Child Safety

  • No public chat or stranger messaging.
  • No child-to-child direct messaging by default.
  • No third-party advertising in the child experience.
  • Parent email, password and payment details are not displayed in child profiles.
  • One parent account can manage a maximum of two child profiles.
  • Parents remain responsible for supervising account use and selecting suitable content for their children.

If a safety issue is found, stop using the affected feature and contact JamKar support through the parent account/help channel when available.

Purchase & Use Terms

Five starter worlds can be tried through Level 3. Full access is currently offered as a £4.99 one-time family purchase for one parent account and up to two child profiles. Payment is processed through Revolut. Access is granted only after JamKar receives and verifies a completed payment from the payment provider.

Do not share parent login credentials with children or unrelated third parties. Users must not attempt to bypass access controls, interfere with the service, scrape account information, abuse other users or use the platform for unlawful purposes.

JamKar may update games, levels, safety controls and technical features over time. If a payment completes but access does not unlock, keep the payment reference and use the support guidance below.

Help

Payment not unlocked: return to JamKar while signed into the same parent account. The site checks the verified server-side entitlement. Do not make repeated payments for the same account.

Forgotten password: use the parent sign-in/account recovery flow provided by the authentication service.

Child profile problem: sign in as the parent and edit the child profiles. Each account supports up to two child usernames.

Security: never send JamKar staff your password, full card number, Supabase service-role key, Revolut API key or webhook signing secret.
